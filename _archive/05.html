If the pre-call video isn't filmed

Don't hold the launch for it. Clone the step, strip the video block, replace it with a short thank-you headline, and publish it as a plain thank-you page. Swap the real video in when it arrives. A Client waiting on their own asset shouldn't cost them a week of spend.
