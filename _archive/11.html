Part B · Section 11

Qualification and DQ

Stopping unqualified prospects from consuming the Client's calendar.

§11.1 Form fields

A strong reference set from a live tax advisory Client:

- Name, email, phone number - Company website - States taxed in - Industry - Services of interest — tax reduction, preparation, accounting, other - Estimated net taxable income, current year, business + personal - Estimated revenue, current year

The income and revenue fields are what the conditional logic keys off. Build the form with the Client on their launch call. Use a single Full Name field rather than splitting first and last, and include the consent checkbox for text messages.

§11.2 DQ page and routing

1. Clone an existing funnel step, rename to DQ, update the path. 2. Strip it to a short headline. Keep the tone professional — this page still represents the Client to someone who may qualify later. 3. Optional: a "what happens next" paragraph with resources, and a closing appreciation line. 4. Publish and copy the URL. 5. In the form, open conditional logic on the income field: if under threshold, redirect to the DQ URL. 6. Alternative: display an inline custom message instead of redirecting. Faster to build, less polished.

Test the logic end to end with a dummy under-threshold submission before go-live.

Set expectations on this

Raising the qualification bar raises cost per booked call, because you're filtering more traffic out. That's expected and is not a signal the campaign is failing. Agree the threshold with the Client explicitly so the resulting number isn't a surprise in week one.
