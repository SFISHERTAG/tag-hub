Asset scripting

Scripting the VSL, the ad, and the pre-call video with ChatGPT.

06.1 One asset at a time
  1. Script each asset as a separate prompt in a separate pass. Batching all three into one request reliably produces worse output — the structure flattens and the results need more rework than the time you saved.
06.2 The modeling prompt
  1. Copy the entire completed intake form.
  2. Paste into ChatGPT with: "Rewrite this, modeling the exact structure of the text I am about to send you."
  3. Wait for it to confirm, then send the proven template for that asset type.
  4. Iterate: feed in the unique mechanism, tighten the callout, make it more assertive, name the specific audience.

This works because output is modeled on structures already proven in market rather than generated from scratch.

06.3 Headline pattern
  1. Three components: an attention statement naming a specific audience, the guarantee, and an aggressive offer paired with an aggressive guarantee. If ChatGPT drifts, give it a direct example in that format rather than describing what you want.
Check before it goes in the builder

Copy is ready when it names a specific audience, states the guarantee, and reflects the Client's approved unique mechanism. Copy written against an unconverted offer — see section 02 — will underperform no matter how good the media buying is.

06.4 Wistia upload standard
  1. Naming. Label the video with the client's business name, not a generic label like "call" or "VSL." This is what keeps the shared Wistia account navigable as the client list grows.
  2. Color code. Go to Appearances and set the player color to the team's standard code (editor's reference is yellow). We pay for Wistia specifically to get branded, trackable players — an unbranded default-blue player defeats the point of the paid account.
  3. Thumbnail. Actually pick one. Don't accept a black frame or a frame that's just floating text. Pick a frame that looks presentable at a glance.
  4. Controls — turn on: Auto Play, Play with Sound, Big Play Button, Full Screen.
  5. Controls — turn off: Control Bar, Play Bar, Captions, Settings.

Why Captions is off: The editor already burns captions into the creative itself. Leaving Wistia's native captions on stacks a second, unstyled caption layer on top of the editor's — turn it off every time.
