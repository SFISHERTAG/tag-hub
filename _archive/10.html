§10.1 Custom values to insert

- Contact → Full Name - Appointment → Start Date and Time - Appointment → Meeting Location

Paste operations frequently drop or mangle these tokens. Re-insert from the Custom Values menu rather than typing them, and read every message before enabling the workflow.

Why both channels every time

If A2P 10DLC registration isn't approved, SMS won't deliver and a text-only sequence fails silently — the Client sees no-shows and no explanation. Email duplication means reminder coverage works on day one regardless. Note: the founder calls this "HTTP" in the recordings. The actual item is A2P 10DLC carrier registration. Search that term, not the other one.

10.2.1 Trigger and calendar
  1. Trigger: Customer Booked Appointment. No extra trigger filters needed beyond the calendar itself.
  2. Name the calendar after the Client's business, never the personal calendar GoHighLevel creates by default — e.g. [Firm Name] Tax Strategy Assessment Call.
  3. Add the Client's logo to the calendar. Small effort, visibly raises perceived quality.
  4. Name the workflow itself after the client, e.g. TAG Reminders or [Firm Name] Reminders.
10.2.2 Immediate confirmation — SMS and email
  1. Build the SMS action first, then the email action directly beneath it in the same workflow step.

SMS — immediate: Hey {{contact.name}}, It's [Company Name]. Thanks for booking a time to speak with us; your call is at {{appointment.start_time}} Meeting Link: {{appointment.meeting_location}} Please reply back with "YES" to confirm you'll show up. Email — immediate: Hey {{contact.name}}, It's [Company Name]. Thanks for booking a time to speak with us; your call is at {{appointment.start_time}} Meeting Link: {{appointment.meeting_location}} Set the sender email to the Client's own business address — it must be added to their GoHighLevel staff list first. Subject line pattern: Important message from [Company Name].

10.2.3 Wait steps and the reminder cadence
  1. After the immediate confirmation, insert a Wait step: Until Scheduled Time → Appointment Time → 12 hours before, with Skip all outbound communications until the next one selected. Repeat this wait-then-send pattern for each interval.

12 hours before: Hey {{contact.name}}, It's [Company Name]. Thanks for booking a time to speak with us; your call is at {{appointment.start_time}} Meeting Link: {{appointment.meeting_location}} 6 hours before: Hey {{contact.name}} just another friendly reminder of our call coming up soon in the next 6 hours. Here's the meeting link just to make it easier! {{appointment.meeting_location}} 3 hours before: Hey {{contact.name}} Just sending the link again just in case you missed it. {{appointment.meeting_location}} 1 hour before: Hey {{contact.name}} I'm hopping on in an hour. Here is the link below! {{appointment.meeting_location}}

Token discipline

Always re-insert {{contact.name}}, {{appointment.start_time}}, and {{appointment.meeting_location}} from the Custom Values menu rather than typing or pasting them — paste operations frequently mangle the braces. Read every message in the workflow before turning it on.

If A2P isn't approved yet

Run every one of these as email-only until approval comes through. Same cadence, same copy, drop the SMS actions.
