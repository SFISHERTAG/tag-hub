The reframe that closes Clients

A $2,000 CAC on a $10,000 client is not $2,000 lost. It's $2,000 deployed to buy $10,000. Once that holds, the only remaining question is how many times it can be repeated — and the answer should be: as many times as the business can handle. That's scaling.

§03.1 Why high ticket wins with paid ads

Ads carry fixed costs, and low-ticket offers break under that pressure. High-ticket offers absorb CAC, fund aggressive testing, scale faster, outbid competitors, and leave more net profit.

Once CAC and ROAS are known, spend becomes predictable, risk becomes calculated, and growth becomes a decision rather than a gamble. Ads stop feeling risky and start functioning as leverage.
