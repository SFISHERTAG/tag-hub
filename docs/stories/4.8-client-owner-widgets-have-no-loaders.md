# Story 4.8 — The client owner dashboard renders nothing it promises

**Status:** Draft — not started, 2026-08-26

## Story

As a client owner signing in, I need my dashboard to render its widgets, so that
the product shows me my own numbers rather than four "not built yet" tiles.

## The defect

`web/src/app/widget-loaders.ts` is the composition root and registers exactly one
feature, `registerClientWidgets`. That file registers four widget ids —
`client_health`, `department_overview`, `portfolio`, `team_health_rollup` — and
all four are internal CSM/CSD surfaces.

`lib/dashboard/widget-definitions.ts` defines eleven ids. Seven have no loader:

```
leads_funnel, spend_roas, kpi_summary, owner_calendar
        offered to client_owner   (widget-definitions.ts :69 :76 :118 :125)
pipeline_board, day_view
        client-facing, but offered to CLIENT_CLOSER / CLIENT_MANAGER, not to
        client_owner              (widget-definitions.ts :55 :62)
team_performance
        internal, and also unregistered
```

`widget-loaders.ts:21` states the consequence in its own comment: a widget id with
no registered loader is not an error, it renders as unbuilt. So a client owner
signing in gets four such tiles, which is their entire dashboard.

**Corrected 2026-08-27. This said six, and six was the count of client-facing
ids rather than the count a client owner is offered.** `CLIENT_OWNER` appears in
exactly four `availableFor` lists. `pipeline_board` and `day_view` are
client-facing and go to closers and managers; an owner never sees either. The
sentence converted "client-facing" into "what a client owner sees" without
saying so, and AC 2 below is scoped to `client_owner`, so an implementer working
to six would register two loaders for a role the criterion does not mention.

The defect is not softened by the correction. Four of four is still the whole
dashboard: every widget a client owner is offered is unregistered, so the
proportion is 100% either way. The number was wrong in the direction that made
it sound worse than a total failure already is.

Three counts live in this story and they are different questions. **Four** is
what a client owner sees. **Six** is the client-facing set, which is this
story's scope. **Seven** adds `team_performance`, which is unregistered and
internal, so it is in scope but not in the user-facing claim.

## Why this is not simply a missing feature

Six of Epic 4's seven stories claim Done. The work they describe exists on the
Next side; it did not cross the Angular migration. So the epic's status is not
wrong about work having been done — it is wrong about that work being reachable.

The Next-side implementations exist under names that do not match their widget
ids: `funnel-chart.tsx` and `funnel-table.tsx` for `leads_funnel`,
`spend-charts.tsx` and `roas-table.tsx` for `spend_roas`, `kpi-tiles.tsx` for
`kpi_summary`, plus `pipeline-board-widget.tsx`, `day-view-widget.tsx` and
`owner-calendar-widget.tsx`. That naming gap is worth stating, because whoever
ports them will search for the widget id and find nothing.

This story exists to make the gap explicit rather than to relitigate those Done
statuses, which belong to their own sessions.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each of the six client-facing ids resolves to a loader, or is removed from the
   registry with its removal recorded. Six, not four: the scope is the
   client-facing set, and `pipeline_board` and `day_view` are in it.
2. A `client_owner` session renders no "not built yet" tile for a widget the
   registry offers it. That is the four of the six carrying `CLIENT_OWNER`, and
   it is the criterion a client owner can actually observe.
3. `team_performance` is decided explicitly: registered, or removed.
4. No sibling-integration import is added. Registration stays in the declared
   composition root, per the CLAUDE.md boundary and its one exemption.
