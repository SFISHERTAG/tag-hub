# Story 2.7 — Call preparation panel

**Epic:** 2 — Closer workspace
**Status:** Draft

## Story

As a closer, I want relevant contact history and attribution surfaced
before a call starts, so that I walk in knowing who I'm talking to without
tabbing to Contacts.

## Context

`/today` (Story 2.2) shows only time, title, and calendar per appointment.
Contact-level attribution and notes already exist on
`/contacts/[contactId]` (Story 2.4); this story brings that context onto
the day view itself rather than requiring a closer to leave it mid-shift.
Every appointment already carries a `contactId` (see the `Appointment` type
in `lib/ghl/appointments.ts`), so the join is straightforward once the
shape of the panel is decided.

## Open questions

Status is Draft, not Ready, because these are unresolved:

- Inline expansion per row, or a separate panel. `/today` is deliberately
  dense; per `docs/architecture.md`, "the closer's [screen] cannot be
  slow," so this needs to not add a data fetch to every row by default.
- Full note history per contact, or just the most recent one or two. Full
  history risks slowing the one screen that has to stay fast.
- Attribution only, or does it also need the deal's pipeline stage and
  value from Story 2.1 / 2.6's data.

## Tasks

None yet. Move to Ready once the open questions above are resolved, then
write acceptance criteria against a specific answer to each.
