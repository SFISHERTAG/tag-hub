

## Correction: this was marked Done while unreachable (resweep High)

Status stays Done, and is now accurate. It was not before.

The full-codebase resweep found `activateCampaignAction` had zero call sites.
Every piece of this story existed and was correct — the Meta unpause, the AP2
stage move, the audit entry, the error messages that distinguish "campaign is
live but the stage move failed" — but nothing in the UI ever called it. The
one button on `/onboarding/launch/activate` submitted Story 5.4's
create-paused action instead, so every campaign launched through the app
stayed paused in Meta indefinitely.

Now wired: `ActivateForm` offers activation as an explicit second step once
the paused campaign exists. It is a separate click on purpose, since
unpausing starts real ad spend. `activateCampaignAction` resolves the
Fulfillment opportunity itself rather than threading an opportunity id
through the launch query string.

Two related changes landed with it. The action is now gated on tenant
ownership and on the campaign belonging to that location (resweep critical
group 1); and `parseFulfillmentStage` replaced the exact stage-name match, so
the AP2 move works against GHL's real stage names.
