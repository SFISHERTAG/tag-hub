

## Post-implementation change: sample-data disclosure (resweep critical 4)

Files this story references were changed by a cross-cutting fix, not by
further work on the story itself. Status is unchanged.

`getMockMetrics()` returns the same four numbers for every client, and those
numbers become the health score, status badge and escalation bucket. They
rendered with no disclosure across the CSM portfolio and the two dashboard
widgets. Now: `ClientHealth.is_sample` marks the values at source,
`portfolio` and `client_health` are in `MOCK_METRICS_WIDGET_IDS`, and
`<SampleDataBanner>` takes the text of what it is disclosing so a health
score is not disclosed as a spend figure. No scoring logic changed.
