# Story 3.2 — Client health signals

**Epic:** 3 — CSM portfolio and impersonation
**Status:** Ready

## Story

As a CSM, I want to see at a glance which clients need attention, so that I can prioritize calls and outreach.

## Context

Health is derived from appointment and conversion data:
- Show rate (showed ÷ booked) — indicates funnel quality
- DQ rate (DQ ÷ showed) — indicates lead quality or targeting issue
- Close rate (closed ÷ showed) — indicates offer/closer fit
- Delivery stalls (deals won but delivery not marked) — indicates churn risk

For MVP, "healthy" is binary: show rate > 30%, no open stalls. This is a default; actual thresholds are dynamically configurable from CSM dashboard (TODO).

## Acceptance criteria

1. A `getClientHealth()` function returns `{ status: "healthy" | "at-risk" | "critical", reason?: string }`.
2. Health is computed from recent outcomes (last 30 days).
3. "At-risk" means show rate 15–30%, or delivery stalls.
4. "Critical" means show rate < 15%.
5. Portfolio (3.1) displays health as a colored badge (green/yellow/red).
