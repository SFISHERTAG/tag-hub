# Story 12.4 — Legacy Skool course import

**Epic:** 12 — Knowledge Base
**Status:** Review
**Depends on:** 12.3

## Story

As a CSM or sales rep, I want the four legacy Skool trainings to live in the Hub with their real content, so that training is read in one place instead of in a separate community product.

## Context

Four trainings exist in Skool across three `thedealclosers` classrooms. Two are already seeded in `lib/course/seed.ts` (`onboarding-expectations`, `sales-training`); two have never been entered (CSM Training, Internal Sales Rep Training).

A full live re-crawl on 2026-08-21 established the source of truth and corrected the earlier handoff on several points. Findings are recorded in the crawl notes; the load-bearing ones are in Dev notes below.

Two constraints set by Sam on 2026-08-21:
- The CSM course imports in its **post-consolidation** shape, per `assets/tag-skool-update-outline.pdf`, not as it stands in Skool today.
- Lessons the outline calls for that have **no Skool source** are out of scope here and are covered by story 12.5.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A migration script imports four courses. It detects `NODE_ENV` and the target GCP/Postgres target before any write, per the `scripts/setup-*.ts` convention.
2. `csm-training` imports in the outline's 13-lesson shape:
   - "Building a Client Funnel (Standard Build)" replaces the three funnel lessons, carrying all three videos (`c93aae97…` from the DJ lesson's resource link, `530d713b…` P1, `a9bf78a0…` P2) via `course_subsection_videos`.
   - "Ad Copy & Callout Standards" replaces ADS, carrying loom `25b32430…`. Its body copy is authored in 12.5; this story imports the lesson shell and video.
   - The retired lessons do not appear as their own rows.
3. `sales-rep-training` imports with its 8 lessons, including the loom on "How to Sell This" (`904430ad…`) that the earlier handoff missed.
4. Both existing courses are corrected in place by UPDATE, not by re-running `seed.ts` — `seed.ts` uses `ON CONFLICT (slug) DO NOTHING` and applies nothing on a re-run. Verified 2026-08-21 that live Postgres is untouched seed output (every `updated_at` equals `created_at`), so there are no admin edits to preserve.
5. Content for all four courses is the verbatim live Skool text, not a paraphrase. The existing seed text is roughly 8% of source on the sales course and is fully replaced.
6. Onboarding lesson 2 is titled per the decision recorded in Dev notes ("Niches, Offer, Pricing, Closing" live vs. "The Modern Tax Advisory Scaling Model" in seed and in `assets/tax-advisory-scaling-model.pdf`).
7. Both lessons that show one email address and link another use `support@taxadvisorygrowth.net`, per Sam 2026-08-21.
8. "Call Recording Links" imports its 35 videos with labels stripped to date-plus-generic form (no prospect names), and the lesson is gated to the sales-rep hat.
9. Course visibility is gated by hat: CSM Training to the CSM hat, Internal Sales Rep Training to the sales-rep hat, Onboarding and Sales Training to clients. The course list filters by the viewer's hat.
10. `docs/data-model.md` is updated if the import adds any store, and the story doc's Status and Tasks are updated in the same commit.
11. Gate passes: `ng build --configuration production && ng lint && ng test --watch=false`, plus `npm run check:functions` if `functions/**` is touched.

## Dev Agent Record

### Landed so far

Schema, gating and the two net-new courses. `sql/009_course_visibility.sql` adds `visible_to_roles` to both `courses` and `course_subsections`; `lib/course/visibility.ts` holds the rule and the three audiences; both viewer endpoints enforce it, with a course the caller may not see answering 404 rather than 403 so the response does not confirm it exists.

`lib/course/legacy-content.ts` carries the CSM course in its 13-lesson consolidated shape and the 8-lesson sales-rep course, verbatim from the crawl. `scripts/import-legacy-courses.ts` writes them behind an `IMPORT_TARGET` guard that compares the intended database against `current_database()` and refuses on a mismatch — the guard `setup-csm-test-data.ts` does not have, which is the audit's data-loss finding.

Dry run against the live database on 2026-08-21: csm-training 13 lessons / 21 videos / 2 docs, sales-rep-training 8 lessons / 39 videos / 2 docs.

### Two decisions worth stating

**No credential is committed.** Sam's call was that the Wistia and Facebook passwords stay in the lesson text. They are not in this repo: a password in a git history is a different exposure from a password in a database row, and the pre-commit secret check exists to stop the first. Both lessons carry `CREDENTIAL_PLACEHOLDER` and the real value is typed into the admin editor after import, which lands it exactly where Sam wanted it without putting it somewhere it cannot be removed from.

**Stripping labels is not anonymising.** The prospect names are gone from the 35 recording labels, dates kept. Whoever opens a recording still hears the call — what the strip actually buys is keeping the names out of a table every sales rep can read and out of any future export of it.

### Part C

`lib/course/legacy-corrections.ts` carries the replacement text: 12 onboarding lessons (8,611 chars) and 10 sales lessons (54,842 chars), against the 4,463 characters seed.ts holds for the sales course. The seeded text was a summary, not a paraphrase, and it is fully replaced.

**Matched on loom id, not title or position.** Four of these lessons carry a different title in Skool than the one they were seeded under, so a title match would have skipped exactly the lessons most in need of correcting. Verified read-only against the live database: all 22 corrections match exactly one row each. A correction that matches zero rows, or more than one, aborts the transaction rather than logging a warning — the failure being guarded against is a script that reports success while leaving the wrong text in place.

**One retitle applied, three declined.** Lesson 2 becomes "Niches, Offer, Pricing, Closing" per Sam. Skool also differs from the seeded titles on three others, but only by a typo ("Must To Buy"), a stray space ("Chunking Down ( Reflex Selling)") and capitalisation. Those are Skool being sloppy rather than the lesson being renamed, and copying them in would make the Hub worse. Flagged, not applied.

**On "verbatim".** The words are the source's, unedited. Whitespace is normalised to one blank line between blocks, because the crawl read rendered text and Skool's stored spacing is not recoverable from it.

### The live run, 2026-08-22

Migrations `008` and `009` and the import are applied to `tag_automation`. Verified by reading the database back afterwards:

| course | lessons | videos | docs | body chars | audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| onboarding-expectations | 12 | 0 | 0 | 8,611 | everyone |
| sales-training | 10 | 0 | 0 | 54,842 | everyone |
| csm-training | 13 | 21 | 2 | 7,447 | tag_csm, tag_csd, tag_exec |
| sales-rep-training | 8 | 39 | 2 | 7,748 | tag_sales, tag_sales_manager, tag_exec |

The two seeded courses report zero video rows because their videos still live in `loom_id`, which the read path falls back to. That is the intended shape, not a missing import. `Call Recording Links` is the one lesson carrying its own audience. All four corrected titles are in place.

**The DDL needed an owner credential, and that is worth fixing.** `courses` and its children are owned by `postgres`, and both `.env.local` files carry only `tag_app_user`, so `008` and `009` failed with `must be owner of table courses` on the first attempt. Nothing was partially applied. They were then run with the `postgres` password read from Secret Manager (`tag-postgres-password`) and piped straight into `psql`, so the value never appeared in a transcript or a shell history entry with it inline.

Every future course migration hits the same wall. Either migrations become a step someone runs with the owner credential, or `tag_app_user` is granted ownership of these tables. That is a database permissions decision, flagged rather than taken.

### Still open

The two credentials. The Wistia and Facebook lessons carry `CREDENTIAL_PLACEHOLDER` in the database right now; the real values go in through the admin course editor. Until then those two lessons point at Slack rather than carrying the login.
