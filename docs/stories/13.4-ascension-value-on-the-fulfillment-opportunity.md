# Story 13.4 — Ascension value on the fulfillment opportunity

**Epic:** 13 — Lifecycle handoff and churn prevention
**Status:** Draft
**Depends on:** 13.3

## Story

As TAG, I want the fulfillment opportunity to carry its ascension value, so that
what a client is worth now is visible where their delivery is tracked.

## Context

The pipeline ends at `AP 5 - Ascension` and nothing populates a value there
today. Every one of the 37 live opportunities sits at `New Client`, so no
ascension has ever been recorded in this system.

Decision taken: ascension value is the opportunity's `monetaryValue`, not a
custom field.

That decision has a consequence worth stating plainly. `monetaryValue` is one
number. If ascension is an *increase* over the original deal, the opportunity
cannot hold both the original and the ascended figure. Either the original value
is carried forward from the prospect opportunity at handoff (13.3 AC1 preserves
it there) and `monetaryValue` on the fulfillment record means current value, or
one of the two numbers is lost. This story assumes current value.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The fulfillment opportunity's `monetaryValue` is the client's current value,
   seeded at handoff from the prospect opportunity's closed value.
2. Updating it is possible from the Hub without entering GHL.
3. `closeOpportunity`'s existing requirement that a won opportunity carries a
   value is respected rather than worked around.
4. Ascension is visible as a change, not just a number. A client whose value
   went up is distinguishable from one that was always at that figure.
5. Portfolio-level total reflects current value across clients.
