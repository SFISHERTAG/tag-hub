# Story 2.8 — Follow-up queue

**Epic:** 2 — Closer workspace
**Status:** Draft

## Story

As a closer, I want a queue of contacts who need follow-up, so that a
no-show or DQ doesn't silently fall through once the day it happened on has
passed.

## Context

Outcome timing is already captured per appointment (Story 2.3,
`AppointmentOutcome` in `lib/ghl/store.ts`), so "marked no-show or DQ and
not since re-booked" is derivable from existing data. Nothing surfaces it
as a worklist today; a closer would have to remember or re-scan `/today`
day by day, which is exactly the kind of manual tracking the Hub exists to
replace.

## Open questions

Status is Draft, not Ready, because these are unresolved:

- The exact definition of "needs follow-up": any no-show or DQ with no
  newer appointment on the same contact, or something narrower.
- Whether entries age out (for example, surfaced for 7 days then dropped)
  or persist until resolved.
- Where it lives: a new route, or a section within `/today`.
- Whether clearing an entry needs an explicit action ("followed up") or is
  implied by booking a new appointment on that contact.

## Tasks

None yet. Move to Ready once the open questions above are resolved, then
write acceptance criteria against a specific answer to each.
