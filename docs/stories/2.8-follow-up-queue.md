# Story 2.8 — Follow-up queue

**Epic:** 2 — Closer workspace
**Status:** Ready

## Story

As a closer, I want a queue of contacts who need follow-up, so that a
no-show or DQ doesn't silently fall through once the day it happened on has
passed.

## Context

Outcome timing is already captured per appointment (Story 2.3,
`AppointmentOutcome` in `lib/ghl/store.ts`), so "marked no-show or DQ and
not since re-booked" is derivable from existing data. Nothing surfaces it
as a worklist today; a closer would have to remember or re-scan `/today`
day by day, which is exactly the kind of manual tracking the Hub exists to
replace.

## Decisions

- Definition of "needs follow-up": an appointment marked no-show, or an
  outcome of not-closed, or an outcome of reschedule — with no newer
  appointment booked on that contact since.
- Aging out: entries age out based on a threshold the sales manager
  defines, either a maximum number of follow-up attempts or an elapsed
  time window. The threshold is configurable, not hardcoded.
- Location: a section within `/today`, not a separate route.
- Clearing: implied automatically once a new appointment is booked on that
  contact — no separate "followed up" action.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `/today` has a follow-up queue section listing contacts whose most
   recent appointment outcome is no-show, not-closed, or reschedule, and
   who have no newer appointment booked since that outcome.
2. A sales manager can configure the aging-out threshold as either a
   maximum number of follow-up attempts or a time window; the default
   behavior before configuration is documented (e.g. no aging out until a
   threshold is set).
3. An entry that exceeds the configured threshold (attempt count or time
   elapsed) is removed from the queue automatically.
4. An entry is removed from the queue as soon as a new appointment is
   booked for that contact — no explicit closer action required.
5. The queue section does not slow `/today`'s initial load; it queries
   only outcome/appointment data already available from Story 2.3, no new
   per-row fetches.
6. If no contacts currently need follow-up, the section shows an empty
   state.
